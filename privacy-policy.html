In short: SizeMint edits your images on your device and does not upload them to the developer. Google services built into the app (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and AdMob) collect certain device and usage information, described below.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SizeMint — Image Compressor & Resizer (“SizeMint”, “the app”), an Android app developed by Muhammad Asher Ansari (“I”, “me”), handles information. SizeMint is an independent app; there is no company behind it, no SizeMint server and no user account.

SizeMint’s image editing (compression, resizing, format conversion and cropping) runs on your device. The app also includes Google services — Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google AdMob with the User Messaging Platform, and the Google ML Kit document scanner — which can collect and transmit certain information as described below. Questions: asheransar@gmail.com.

2. Information the app accesses

  • Images you choose. You select images with the Android photo picker (or the system document picker on older devices), or share them to SizeMint from another app. SizeMint can read only the images you pick or share; it does not request access to your whole photo library, storage, camera, location or contacts.
  • Documents you scan with the “Scan Document” feature (see section 3).
  • Your settings (theme, dynamic color, default quality and output format).
  • Device and usage information collected by the Google SDKs listed in sections 5–8.

3. Image processing and document scanning

Image editing. Compression, resizing, conversion and cropping are performed on your device by SizeMint’s own code. SizeMint does not upload your photos or documents to the developer or to any server operated for SizeMint. Location, camera and other EXIF metadata are not copied into the new files SizeMint creates; your original files are never modified.

Document scanning. “Scan Document” opens Google’s ML Kit Document Scanner, which is provided by Google Play services and shows Google’s own scanner screens (camera capture or gallery import, edge detection, cropping and filters). Google Play services may need to download the scanner component the first time you use it. According to Google’s ML Kit documentation, ML Kit APIs process images on the device. The scanner’s use is governed by Google’s terms and privacy policy. SizeMint receives the resulting JPEG pages and PDF and stores them locally (section 4).

4. Local file storage

The following stays on your device and is not sent to the developer:

  • Settings, stored in the app’s private storage.
  • Export history: file name, file location on your device, format, original and output size, dimensions and date for each image or PDF you save. You can remove individual entries in History. History is excluded from Android backup.
  • Temporary files (processed images and scanned pages/PDFs) in the app’s cache. They are deleted automatically after about a day or when newer sessions replace them, and Android may clear the cache at any time.
  • Files you save go to your device’s Pictures/SizeMint or Downloads/SizeMint folder (Android 10+) or to a location you choose (Android 8–9). These are your files; uninstalling SizeMint does not delete them.

5. Firebase Analytics

SizeMint includes Google Firebase Analytics with its default configuration. SizeMint does not log custom events. Firebase Analytics automatically collects information such as app-instance identifiers, automatically collected events (for example first open, session start and in-app screen views), app version, device model, operating-system version, language and country/region derived from your IP address, and — where available — the device advertising identifier. Google uses this to provide usage statistics to the developer. See Google’s documentation: Data collection in Google Analytics for Firebase and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

6. Firebase Crashlytics

SizeMint includes Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports. When the app crashes, Crashlytics may send the crash stack trace, a Crashlytics installation identifier, device model, operating-system version, app version and device state at the time of the crash (such as free memory and orientation). Crash reports help fix bugs. Your images are not included. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

7. Google AdMob

SizeMint is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (banner ads at the bottom of some screens and occasional full-screen ads after a task is completed). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as the advertising identifier, IP address (from which approximate location may be derived), app interaction information (for example ad views and taps), diagnostic information and device information, to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. See Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

8. Advertising identifiers

The app declares Android’s advertising-ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and the Android Privacy Sandbox ad-services permissions through the Google SDKs. These allow Google’s SDKs to use the advertising identifier for advertising and analytics. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s settings (for example Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your device).

9. Consent and privacy choices

SizeMint uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where required by law or Google policy (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and certain U.S. states), a consent or privacy message configured in the developer’s AdMob account is shown, and ads are requested only after the consent framework allows it. If a privacy options entry is required for your region, you can review or change your choice at any time in SizeMint › Settings › “Ad privacy choices”.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics currently run with their default settings and are not controlled by the ad consent message.

11. Data sharing

  • With Google: the information described in sections 5–8 is sent directly by Google’s SDKs to Google.
  • By you: when you use Share (an image, PDF, or Share App), Android’s share sheet sends the selected file or text to the app you choose. That app’s privacy policy then applies.
  • Not sold by the developer: the developer does not sell your personal information and does not receive your images or documents.

12. Data retention and deletion

  • Local data (settings, history, cache) remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app. You can delete history entries in History and clear the app’s storage in Android settings.
  • Files you saved stay in your device folders until you delete them.
  • Data collected by Google’s SDKs is retained according to Google’s policies and the retention settings available to the developer in Firebase and AdMob. The developer has no SizeMint server and holds no copy of your images.

13. Data security

Local data is kept in SizeMint’s private app storage, which Android protects from other apps. Exported files are written to locations you choose and are accessible as normal files on your device. Google states that its SDKs transmit data over encrypted connections (see the Google documents linked above). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

14. Children’s privacy

SizeMint is a general utility app and is not directed at children. It is not designed for or intentionally marketed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact asheransar@gmail.com.

15. User rights and choices

  • Choose which images to open; SizeMint cannot read others.
  • Change ad consent in Settings › “Ad privacy choices” when it is required for your region.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in device settings.
  • Remove history entries, clear app data, or uninstall the app to delete local data.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because the developer does not hold your images or account data, requests about data collected by Google’s SDKs may also be directed to Google. You can contact the developer at the address below.

16. International data processing

Information collected by Google’s SDKs may be processed by Google in countries other than your own, including the United States, under Google’s data protection terms.
